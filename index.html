At a time you choose, Qalil pauses your distracting apps until you’ve read a little Quran.

Qalil (قليل) means “a little”.

How it works

  1. 1 Pause

    At your daily time, the apps you picked are paused with Apple’s Screen Time.

  2. 2 Read

    Open Qalil and read the Quran for a few minutes. Five, to begin with.

  3. 3 Return

    Your apps unlock for the rest of the day. Keep it up, and the target grows gently each week.
